Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Torchons & Serviettes is designed to collect the bare minimum. No e-mail, no password, no phone number, no geolocation, no ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics.

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails as the legally binding text.

3. No tracking, no cookies

The app embeds no audience analytics tool, no third-party crash reporter, no advertising tracker. This website sets no cookies and loads no external resources (fonts, scripts, CDN).

Direct consequence of this choice: no bug is reported automatically. Fixing issues therefore relies on user reports — by e-mail to contact@torchonsetserviettes.app or via the public repository's issues (link added on opening). See the Contact page.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

The app's data is hosted in France (Scaleway). One transfer outside the EU concerns the delivery of push notifications via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (United States). This transfer is governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. The content transmitted is limited to what's strictly necessary to display a notification. This website is delivered by Cloudflare (a US company), and the Android backup and connectivity test described in section 2 are Google's: they follow their own policies.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app (Settings › Delete my account). Here is what happens, as the server does it:

  • Immediately: you leave all your households, the account is marked deleted, and every phone connected to the account is signed out and erases its local data on its next call to the server.
  • For 30 days: your recovery code ("Recover my household") lets you come back and restore everything.
  • After 30 days (nightly job): the account is anonymized, not removed from the database. Your name becomes "Former member", the recovery code is erased (no return possible any more), and your devices, notification tokens, personal settings, streaks and personal tasks are deleted. The tasks you completed stay in the household history, under the name "Former member".
  • Households: a household with no active member left is archived by the next nightly job, then permanently deleted 365 days later, with its tasks, history, presence calendar and the associated home Wi-Fi names. While the household exists, those last two items are not erased by an account deletion alone.

The server's technical logs (IP addresses) are not tied to an account and are not affected by account deletion.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability regarding your data.

  • Erasure: can be done autonomously from the app (Settings › Delete my account); the exact effects, including anonymization after 30 days, are described in section 6.
  • Other rights: exercise them by writing to contact@torchonsetserviettes.app. A reply will be provided within the legal time limits.

You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority CNIL (www.cnil.fr) if you believe the processing is not compliant.

8. Security

No password is stored (the app does not use one). The recovery code is kept only as an argon2id hash, never in clear. Communications between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Minimum age

The app is intended for people aged 13 or older. It is not designed for children below this age and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

This policy may evolve (new feature, change of processor). In case of a material change, the "last updated" date at the top of the page is refreshed. Major changes may be flagged in the app.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: contact@torchonsetserviettes.app.

12. Your connected devices

You can see at any time which devices hold a session on your account, and cut off access for any of them: Settings › Household › My devices. A cut-off device is signed out and its local data is erased the next time it connects. A device that never reconnects keeps what it already holds in memory: no app can erase a phone that stays offline, and we would rather say so than let you assume otherwise.
