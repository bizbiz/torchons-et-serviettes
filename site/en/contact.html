Contact

A question, a bug, a suggestion, or a request about your personal data? Here's how to reach the developer.

E-mail

contact@torchonsetserviettes.app
This is also the address to exercise your GDPR rights (see the privacy policy). Typical reply within a few days — solo project.

Report a bug / suggest an idea

The app sends no automatic crash reports — it's a privacy choice (see the privacy policy). In return, bugs are only visible if you report them: your feedback is the only way to spot and fix them.

Two channels, your choice:

  • By e-mail: contact@torchonsetserviettes.app — the simplest, works right now.
  • Via the public repository's issues (open-source code) — the link will be added here when the repository opens; in the meantime, the e-mail above works just fine.

Supporting the project

The app is free and will remain so within its current scope. Buying a coffee is entirely optional and unlocks no feature — a support link will be added here soon.
