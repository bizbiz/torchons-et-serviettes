Torchons & Serviettes helps a household — couple, flat-share, family — share the chores so the same person isn't always the one thinking about them. Recurrence, priorities, fair share, personal preferences.

What the app does

  • Recurring tasksTasks that come back (every X days, on Monday, on the 1st of the month…) shared across the household.
  • Urgency at a glanceGreen / orange / red: see right away what's urgent and what can wait.
  • Fair shareA configurable ratio per member: the load can be fair, not just equal.
  • Personal preferencesOne prefers vacuuming, the other dishes: the app takes that into account when sharing tasks.
  • EffortEach task has an "effort" score: 5 min of dishes ≠ a deep clean, and the math knows it.
  • Away handlingEveryone's away for 2 weeks? The app won't ask you to vacuum on the way back.
  • OfflineWorks without a network and syncs by itself as soon as the connection is back.
  • No account, no trackingNo e-mail, no password. No ads, no trackers, no third-party analytics.
